This page explains what the restaurant data and recommendations on SrinagarEats represent, and their limits. It applies to the main guide and every guide page under /srinagar-restaurants/.

Information Only

Everything on this site, ratings, reviews, addresses, hours, prices, and recommendations, is provided for general informational purposes. It is not professional advice, and nothing here is an endorsement, partnership, or paid placement unless explicitly stated.

Details Can Change

Restaurant ratings, review counts, opening hours, menus, and prices all change over time and are not verified in real time. Always confirm hours and availability directly with a restaurant, especially before a special trip, and treat this site as a starting point for research, not a live source of truth.

Closed or Incorrect Listings

The underlying data is refreshed periodically, not continuously, so a restaurant that has closed, moved, or changed hands may still appear here until the next refresh. If you find a listing that's wrong or a restaurant that's closed, please use the feedback form to flag it, corrections are made from real reports like these.

What's Sourced vs. What's Written

Ratings, review counts, addresses, phone numbers, and amenity tags (delivery, takeout, etc.) come directly from Google Places and are not edited or interpreted. Restaurant descriptions and the praise/complaint themes on the Insights page are synthesized by an AI model from real review text, the model is instructed to only draw from that text, not invent claims, but AI-generated summaries can occasionally miss nuance. Long-form guide posts (like Where to Get Real Wazwan) are written and fact-checked by hand against the same underlying data. Full methodology is documented on the Insights page.

No Guarantee of Accuracy

While every effort is made to keep this data accurate and sourced honestly, SrinagarEats makes no guarantee that any listing is complete, current, or error-free, and isn't liable for decisions made based on it (a bad meal, a closed restaurant, a wrong address). Community-submitted tips are reviewed before publishing but reflect the submitter's personal opinion, not a verified fact.
