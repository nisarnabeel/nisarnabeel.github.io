Why This Site Exists

Growing up in Srinagar, I kept running into the same problem looking for where to eat: generic "top 10" articles written once and never updated, or aggregators cluttered with duplicate listings. I work with data professionally, so I pointed that at something closer to home: real Google Places ratings and reviews, analyzed properly.
