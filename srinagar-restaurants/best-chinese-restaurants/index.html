Srinagar Food Guide

Best Chinese Restaurants in Srinagar

Srinagar has a real, distinct Chinese-Tibetan food scene, much of it built by the city's Tibetan refugee community around Badaamwari and the Boulevard Road strip near Dal Lake. Here are the 18 restaurants tagged for Chinese cuisine in our dataset, ranked by actual rating and review volume, not by who has the best photos.

18 restaurants · 4.38★ average rating · 7,510 combined reviews. See methodology at the bottom.

"Chinese food" in Srinagar usually means Indo-Chinese and Tibetan cooking side by side on the same menu: chilli chicken and chow mein next to thukpa and momos. The city's Tibetan Colony in Badaamwari and the stretch of Boulevard Road facing Dal Lake in Durgjan are the two real clusters; most of the restaurants below sit in one or the other.

#1 · Best all-rounder

Bistro Boulevard by Pincer Foods

4.5★ · 1,619 reviews · Durgjan

A casual cafe right on Boulevard Road with a Dal Lake view, mixing Chinese dishes into a wider Kashmiri and continental menu. Highest review count of any genuinely mixed-cuisine spot on this list, which matters. A menu this broad only holds a 4.5★ average at 1,600+ reviews if the kitchen is consistently good across categories, not just one dish.

Known for: rogan josh, yakhni, plus a Chinese section on the menu; also does desserts like a chocolate croissant

Opp. Ghat No 9, Boulevard Road, near Hotel New Park, Durgjan, Srinagar, 190001

#2 · Most-reviewed

Lhasa

4.0★ · 2,289 reviews · Durgjan

The most-reviewed Chinese kitchen in Srinagar by a wide margin, and a long-standing name on Boulevard Road. The rating sits lower than others on this list at 4.0★, and reviews are genuinely mixed on food quality, but the sheer review volume means it's been a default choice for locals for years. Go for the classics (chicken chilli, chow mein) rather than anything adventurous.

Known for: chicken chilli gravy, chicken chow mein

Lane No 3, Boulevard Road, Near Shah Abbas Hotel, Durgjan, Srinagar, 190001

#3 · Best fusion menu

Fat Panda

4.3★ · 766 reviews · Durgjan

Also on the Durgjan/Boulevard strip, but leans further into a broader Asian menu: Chinese, Thai, and pizza on the same card. Reviewers consistently call out generous portions on dishes like hot and sour soup and Korean fried chicken, and repeatedly name-check a specific host (Suhail) for hospitality, a good sign of a place that's run with real attention, not just processed through.

Known for: hot and sour soup, fried Korean chicken

Opposite Ghat No. 5, Boulevard Rd, Durgjan, Srinagar, 190001

#4 · Tibetan Colony specialist

Chopsticks

4.2★ · 572 reviews · Badaamwari

Sits in the Tibetan Colony in Badaamwari rather than the touristy Boulevard strip, the other real cluster of Chinese-Tibetan cooking in the city. Reviewers repeatedly single out the momos specifically as "delightful," though several more recent reviews flag longer waits and inconsistency on fried rice and chilli chicken. Go for the momos, not necessarily everything else on the menu.

Known for: momos

4R56+JR, Tibetan Colony, Badaamwari, Srinagar, 190003

#5 · Best thukpa and momos

Tibetan Bowl

4.0★ · 585 reviews · Lal Chowk

Closer to the city center on Residency Road, near Lal Chowk. Reviewers are specific and consistent here: gravy momos, shredded mutton egg rice, and multiple thukpa varieties all get named directly, in a setting people repeatedly describe as clean and modern. A few reviews flag pricing on the higher side for what you get.

Known for: Tibetan gravy momos, shredded mutton egg rice, thukpa

Residency Road, near Canara Bank, Regal Chowk, Lal Chowk, Srinagar, 190001

#6 · Highest rated with real volume

Café Tibet

4.5★ · 207 reviews · Press Colony

Tucked on the first floor of the Khidmat complex near Regal Chowk, easy to walk past. Multiple reviewers independently call the momos "the best in Srinagar," which is a strong claim to see repeated across separate reviews rather than once. Also gets specific praise for Chicken Thai Pai and thukpa, and for staff staying attentive even close to closing time.

Known for: momos, Chicken Thai Pai, thukpa

1st floor, Khidmat Complex, Regal Lane, Residency Road, Press Colony, Srinagar, 190001

Worth knowing: A meaningful share of Srinagar's "Chinese food" scene is really run by the city's Tibetan community, especially around Badaamwari's Tibetan Colony. That's why so many places on this list serve Chinese and Tibetan dishes (momos, thukpa) on the same menu rather than treating them as separate cuisines.
